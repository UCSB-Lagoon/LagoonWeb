# Lagoon — Market Research

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (UCSB-specific)
- **UCSB Official App / GOLD Portal** — clunky, web-based, no unified experience. Students hate it.
- **UCSBGlobal** — limited to international student e-forms, not a daily-use app.
- **No known UCSB-specific super app exists** — Lagoon has a clear first-mover advantage.

### Indirect Competitors (general student tools)
- **MyStudyLife** — generic schedule planner, no campus integration, no dining/events.
- **Navigate360** — institutional tool pushed by universities, clinical UX, no student love.
- **Syracuse OrangeNow** — university-official companion app, 4.7 stars but only 46 ratings. Low engagement ceiling.
- **Notion / Google Calendar** — DIY setups students cobble together; Lagoon replaces the need.

### Competitive Moat
Lagoon's direct GOLD sync + real-time Carrillo/De La Guerra/Ortega/Portola menus + AS finance transparency is impossible for a generic app to replicate. UCSB exclusivity is a strength, not a limitation — it enables deep integration no outside tool can match.

## Target Audience Behavior

- **Primary:** UCSB undergrads (18–22), iPhone-first, TikTok/Instagram natives
- **Secondary:** Transfer students and incoming freshmen — highest pain point with fragmented tools
- **Behavior patterns:**
  - Check dining menus daily before biking across campus
  - Rely on word-of-mouth (GroupMe, iMessage threads, Discord) for app recommendations
  - Heavily active on Reddit (r/UCSantaBarbara), Instagram, and TikTok
  - Respond to peer credibility — "built by Gauchos" messaging resonates strongly
- **Key insight:** Students don't search for apps — they hear about them from friends or see them go viral on social. **Referral and social virality are the primary acquisition channels.**
