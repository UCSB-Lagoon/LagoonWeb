**Q: What time does UCSB dining close?**
A: Most dining commons serve dinner until 8:30pm on weekdays and weekends. Ortega (takeout only) closes at 8pm Monday through Friday and is closed on weekends.

**Q: Does UCSB dining have vegan options?**
A: Yes. All four UCSB dining commons label vegan (vgn) and vegetarian (v) items on their menus daily. DLG's Greens salad bar and Portola's plant-forward stations are particularly strong for plant-based eaters.

**Q: How do I check the UCSB dining menu today?**
A: You can view today's menu at apps.dining.ucsb.edu/menu/day, or use the Lagoon app, which shows live menus for all four dining commons alongside your class schedule and campus events.

**Q: Which UCSB dining hall is the best?**
A: It depends on what you're in the mood for. Carrillo is known for its pizza oven, bakery, and themed international dinners. DLG's Taqueria is a student favorite. Portola is the largest and most varied. Ortega is best when you need a quick takeout meal between classes.

**Q: Can I use dining swipes at all dining commons?**

- **Writer guidance:** Write from a student perspective with first-hand familiarity. Avoid generic phrasing ("UCSB is a prestigious university..."). Get specific about campus geography and station names — this signals authenticity to both readers and Google.
