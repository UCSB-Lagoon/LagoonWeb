What is Lagoon?

Lagoon is a free iOS app for UCSB students that combines class schedules, dining menus, events, Daily Nexus news, and grade data in one place.

Who are these guides for?

They are written for newly admitted students, freshmen, current UCSB students, and anyone trying to understand how UCSB systems like dining and GOLD actually work.

What makes Lagoon useful once classes start?

It puts schedules, dining menus, campus events, Daily Nexus news, and grade data in one place, which makes everyday campus life feel less fragmented.
