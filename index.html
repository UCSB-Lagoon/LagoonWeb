Common questions

Everything you need to know about Lagoon

What is Lagoon? +
Lagoon is a free iOS app built exclusively for UCSB students. It brings together your GOLD class schedule, live dining menus for all four dining commons, campus events, Daily Nexus news, and historical grade distributions — all in one beautifully designed native app.
How do I get it? +
Lagoon is available as a public beta on TestFlight for iOS 17 and later. Tap the Download button at the top of this page, or go directly to the TestFlight link. It's free to join — no waitlist, no invite needed.
Does Lagoon show live UCSB dining menus? +
Yes. Lagoon shows live menus for all four UCSB dining commons — Carrillo, De La Guerra, Portola, and Ortega — updated in real time throughout the day. You can see what's being served, dietary labels, and whether each hall is currently open or closed.
Can it sync my GOLD schedule? +
Yes. Lagoon pulls in your enrolled classes from GOLD and displays them in a clean, color-coded weekly view. It also supports iOS home screen and lock screen widgets so your next class is always one glance away.
Is it official? Is it safe? +
Lagoon is student-built and independent — not an official UCSB product. It uses publicly available UCSB data (dining menus, events, grade distributions) and your GOLD credentials only to sync your personal schedule. Your data is never sold or shared.
What about the social features? +
Social features — including Compare Schedules, Shared Class Finder, and Share Cards — are actively in development and coming soon. They'll let you find Gauchos in your classes, compare free time, and share your quarter as a polished card to your story.
