The Company

Built by Gauchos who wanted a better default app for UCSB.

Lagoon exists because campus life at UCSB was too fragmented. Students were switching between separate systems for schedules, dining, events, news, and planning, even though those things all shape the same day.

  • Student-built and focused on the real rhythm of life at UCSB.
  • Designed to feel calm, useful, and local instead of generic campus software.
  • Starting with UCSB first so the product can feel truly native to Gauchos.
  • Free to use, with a simpler and more connected student experience as the goal.

Quick Answers

What students usually want to know first

What is Lagoon?

Lagoon is a free iOS app built for UCSB students. It brings together schedules, dining menus, campus events, Daily Nexus news, and grade data in one place.

Is Lagoon free?

Yes. Lagoon is free to use.

Is Lagoon official?

No. Lagoon is student-built and independent, designed around how Gauchos actually move through campus life.

Are there also student guides on the site?

Yes. Lagoon also publishes UCSB guides for freshmen, dorm life, dining, GOLD, class planning, and other questions students usually have.
