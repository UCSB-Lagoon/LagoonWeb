Week one

UCSB first week guide: what matters most once classes begin.

The first week is not about mastering everything. It is about turning UCSB from an idea into an actual routine. Learn your routes, get food without overthinking it, and make the week feel more familiar by the end of Friday.

First week Freshmen Campus routine

Your goals for week one

  1. Know where your classes are without panic.
  2. Have one or two easy dining options.
  3. Recognize a few faces and start basic routines.
  4. Keep the week small instead of trying to optimize your entire life immediately.
