Housing

Best dorms at UCSB: what “best” actually means.

The best dorm is not automatically the nicest-looking one. It is the one that fits your priorities: convenience, social life, quieter routines, or feeling close to the parts of campus you will actually use.

Best dorms Housing Freshmen

How to think about dorms more usefully

  • Ask what kind of week you want, not just what looks best in a ranking.
  • Think about distance to class, food, and the pace you like.
  • Remember that your social experience depends on you too, not only the building.
If you wantPay attention to
ConvenienceHow the location fits your likely class and dining routine
More social energyThe kind of community and rhythm you want around you
Less frictionHow easy it will be to settle into normal everyday life
The honest answer

There usually is not one universally best dorm. There is a best match for the kind of first year you want to have and how much structure or energy you want around you.
