BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

A few good
questions.

What is Lagoon?

Lagoon is the free all-in-one campus app for UCSB students. It combines GOLD schedule sync, real grade distribution data, live dining commons menus, campus events, Daily Nexus headlines, classmate discovery, and study groups in one app.

Is Lagoon free?

Yes. Lagoon is free to download on the App Store and there are no paid tiers.

Does Lagoon sync with UCSB GOLD?

Yes. Lagoon imports your registered courses from UCSB GOLD so your schedule, attendance, and finals countdown stay accurate.

How do I import my UCSB schedule into Lagoon?

Snap a screenshot of your GOLD schedule and Lagoon reads it automatically — or sign in to GOLD, or paste your class list. Import takes about 30 seconds, and you get a Today view plus home-screen widgets.

Where does Lagoon's grade data come from?

Grade distributions are aggregated from UCSB's publicly released grade reports — including average GPA, A-rate, and D/F-rate for over 500 courses.

Who can use Lagoon?

Lagoon is built exclusively for UCSB undergraduates and graduate students. Sign-in requires a valid UCSB account.

Is Lagoon affiliated with UCSB?

Lagoon is an independent, student-built app for the UCSB community. It is not an official university service.
