## What helps with GE planning

-   Know which categories are still open.
-   Look for classes that fit your schedule instead of treating GE planning like a puzzle for its own sake.
-   Use GEs to balance hard quarters when possible.
-   Keep checking progress so you do not create surprises for future-you.

## What makes GE planning harder than it needs to be

Waiting too long, picking random classes with no strategy, or forgetting that GEs are still part of your overall energy and workload. A GE can help your quarter or make it more annoying.
