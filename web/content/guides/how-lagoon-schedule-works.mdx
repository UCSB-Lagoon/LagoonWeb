## Screenshot → import → your week

1. Open your registered schedule in [UCSB GOLD](https://my.sa.ucsb.edu/gold/) and take a screenshot with your classes, days and times visible.
2. Open Lagoon and choose screenshot import. Screenshot reading happens on your phone.
3. Review the imported classes, times and rooms. Your week is ready to use.

Prefer another method? Lagoon also supports GOLD sign-in and pasting your class list.

## What you get

- **Today:** your next class, time and room.
- **Quarter:** your weekly schedule and finals context.
- **Home-screen widgets:** your next class without opening the app.
- **People:** see who shares your classes and compare schedules with friends.

## Changed a class in GOLD?

Re-import your schedule after adding or dropping a class, then check the result. GOLD remains the official source for your registration; a screenshot reflects the schedule at the time you took it.
