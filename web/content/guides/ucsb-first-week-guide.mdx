## Your goals for week one

1.  Know where your classes are without panic.
2.  Have one or two easy dining options.
3.  Recognize a few faces and start basic routines.
4.  Keep the week small instead of trying to optimize your entire life immediately.

## What usually helps most

| Area | Best move |
| --- | --- |
| Classes | Get there early once so the rest of the quarter feels easier. |
| Food | Figure out your easiest lunch and dinner fallback options fast. |
| Social life | Talk to the people you already keep seeing. |
| Stress | Judge the week by whether it is getting more familiar, not whether you feel perfect. |
