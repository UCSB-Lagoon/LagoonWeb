## The Fall 2026 dates that actually matter

| Date | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday, September 20, 2026 | Fall Quarter 2026 officially begins |
| Monday–Wednesday, September 21–23 | Pre-instruction days — orientation events, settling in, no classes yet |
| Thursday, September 24, 2026 | **Instruction begins — your first day of class** |
| Thursday–Friday, November 26–27, 2026 | Thanksgiving holiday |
| Friday, December 4, 2026 | Instruction ends |
| Saturday–Friday, December 5–11, 2026 | Final exams |

Dates come from UCSB's academic calendar. Always double-check against the official [Registrar academic calendar](https://registrar.sa.ucsb.edu/calendars/academic-calendars) before booking travel — the university, not this page, is the source of truth.

## "Quarter begins" vs. "instruction begins" — what's the difference?

UCSB's calendar lists two start dates, and they confuse almost everyone the first time. The quarter *officially* begins on Sunday, September 20 — that is an administrative date. Nothing happens in a lecture hall that day.

Instruction — actual classes — begins the following Thursday, September 24. The Monday through Wednesday in between are pre-instruction days: dorm life starts, orientation events run, and campus slowly fills up. Yes, UCSB really does start your first week of college on a Thursday. You get a two-day week to find your classrooms before your first full week hits.

## What should you do between move-in and your first class?

Those pre-instruction days are the best free time you will get all quarter. The students who use them well walk into week one noticeably calmer.

-   Walk your class route once. Actually find the lecture halls — UCSB's campus is bigger than the map makes it look, and an 8am is a bad time to discover that.
-   Get your schedule out of GOLD and into something you will actually look at every day.
-   Eat at a dining commons or two so your first hungry moment between classes isn't a research project.
-   Say yes to the awkward dorm-hall events. Everyone is equally new for exactly one week — it is the easiest social window of the year.

## How long is fall quarter?

Ten weeks of instruction plus finals week. It moves faster than a semester — midterms can start as early as week three, and Thanksgiving lands in week nine, right before the end-of-quarter crunch. The quarter system's honest trade: you're never bored, and you're never fully caught up.

If you want to get ahead of that rhythm, the [first week guide](/ucsb-first-week-guide) covers how to set up a week that survives contact with reality.

## When should you plan to arrive?

Move-in for university housing is scheduled by UCSB Housing and typically happens during the days right before instruction begins — your exact slot comes from the housing portal, not the academic calendar. Book travel around your assigned move-in date, and leave yourself at least one full pre-instruction day on campus if you can.
