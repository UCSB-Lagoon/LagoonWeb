Lagoon is a student-built app for UCSB. Your schedule, dining menus, classmates, and campus — together in one place.

A normal day.
Fewer moving parts.

Check GOLD. Find a classroom. Figure out lunch. See what your friends are doing. These are all parts of the same day, but they rarely live in the same place.

We’re building Lagoon to bring them together, with the details that make sense at UCSB: dining commons menus, real grade distributions, campus events, and the people in your classes.
