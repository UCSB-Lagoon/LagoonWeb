UCSB finals week guide: how to make the week feel less chaotic.

Updated: May 2026

Finals week gets harder when every exam feels equally urgent all the time. A better approach is visibility, pacing, and deciding what matters most on each day instead of panicking at the whole week at once.

Finals week Study planning Pacing

What helps most during finals

  • Knowing your exam schedule clearly before the week starts.
  • Breaking study blocks by priority, not by panic.
  • Protecting sleep enough that your brain still works.
  • Choosing a couple of reliable study spots instead of wandering.

How to avoid the usual finals spiral

Common problemBetter move
Trying to work on everything equallyRank by urgency and impact
Losing hours to uncertaintyStart with the next most useful block of work
Studying in random places every dayPick reliable spots and keep moving
